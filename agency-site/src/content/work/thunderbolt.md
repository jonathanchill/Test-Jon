## The brief

Thunderbolt is a digital agency in Farnham that helps growing businesses with web design, SEO, paid search, social, email and branding. Its SEO clients had started asking a new question: why does ChatGPT recommend our competitor and not us? Thunderbolt wanted a credible answer, a service it could sell, and the tooling to deliver it at agency scale.

At the same time, monthly client reporting across six channels was consuming a day or more per account manager.

## What we did

- **AI Search Visibility programme.** We built Thunderbolt a repeatable audit: a benchmark of how each client brand shows up across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity for the questions buyers actually ask, a technical checklist covering crawlability, structured data and `llms.txt`, and a content plan that targets the gaps. The audit runs monthly so progress is measurable.
- **White-labelled tooling.** The benchmark runs as a tool inside Thunderbolt's own stack, branded as theirs, with an evaluation set so the team can trust the scores.
- **Reporting automation.** Ad platform, analytics, search console and social data now flow into one place. A drafting assistant writes the first pass of each monthly report in Thunderbolt's tone of voice, and the account manager finishes it.
- **Adoption.** Workshops for the SEO and account teams, a playbook for selling the new service, and office hours for the first two months.

## The result

AI Search audits became a new line on the rate card and sold to fourteen existing clients in the first quarter. Across those clients, the share of brands cited in AI-generated answers rose from roughly one in eight to more than four in ten within four months. Account managers got the better part of a day back each week, which went into the strategic work clients actually pay for.

> "Our clients were asking about AI search before we had an answer. Now it's one of the first things we talk about in a pitch."
> Founder, Thunderbolt
